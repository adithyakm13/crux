# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be direct about the work and respectful about the people. Technical criticism of
a design, a number or a piece of code is welcome and expected. Personal attacks,
harassment, and demeaning or discriminatory conduct are not.

Concretely, what is expected:

- Critique the argument, not the author.
- Bring evidence. "This is wrong" is not useful; "this is wrong, here is the
  failing case" is.
- Assume competence and good faith, including from people whose first language
  is not English.
- Accept a decision once it has been made and argued, or reopen it with new
  evidence rather than by repetition.

What is not acceptable: harassment of any kind, sexualised language or imagery,
insults, sustained disruption, publishing others' private information, and any
conduct that would reasonably make someone unwilling to participate.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues through GitHub's private report flow on `cruxci/crux`, or
to the maintainers directly. Reports are handled confidentially and answered
within 5 working days.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that violate this document,
and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
